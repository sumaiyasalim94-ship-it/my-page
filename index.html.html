What is ESD?

Endoscopic Submucosal Dissection is an advanced endoscopic technique that enables en bloc removal of early gastrointestinal lesions, with organ preservation.

✅ One-piece removal✅ Lower recurrence✅ Organ preservation✅ Shorter recovery

Indications

Early GI cancersLarge precancerous polypsSelected submucosal tumorsSuitable GI lesions

Latest clinical evidence (tap to open)

Local recurrence after colorectal ESD

Bani Fawwaz et al. (2026), Gastrointestinal Endoscopy. Large international Western multicenter study. Local recurrence 1.5% (22/1,478) among patients with surveillance data.
DOI: 10.1016/j-gie.2026.06.064

ESD vs EMR: systematic review

Bhat et al. (2025), Proc (Baylor Univ Med Center). 25 studies, 5,283 patients: higher complete and en bloc resection with ESD; no significant difference in recurrence.
DOI: 10.1080/08998280.2025.2583724
